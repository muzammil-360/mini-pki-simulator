How the simulator works

  1. Root CACreates an RSA key pair and signs its own certificate. It is the trust anchor.
  2. Intermediate CAThe Root signs the Intermediate's certificate. The Root then stays offline.
  3. User certificateThe Intermediate signs a user's name, email and public key.
  4. Verify chainCheck signatures up to a trusted Root, plus dates and revocation.
  5. RevokeThe CA publishes a signed revocation list (CRL) of serial numbers.

Everything runs in your browser with real RSA-2048 and SHA-256. Learn more: RFC 5280 (X.509 and CRL)
